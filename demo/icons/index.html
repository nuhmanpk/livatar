Animated icons

Brand icons for socials, dev platforms, and sponsors that move. They are plain SVG with the animation inside, so they play in <img> tags, GitHub READMEs, and markdown. Click any icon for copy-ready snippets.

Living UI icons

Characters as interface icons: a sun and moon theme toggle that follows your cursor, plus UI toggles like theme, like and favorite. Hover or tap one to see it react, and click any tile for copy-ready JS, React, Next.js and AI agent code.

📱 Tap to react, drag a finger across the row, or tilt your phone to make them look around.
